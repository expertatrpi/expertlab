Reliability testing platform and method

    Details
  • Mission profile based electro-thermal stress platform for reliability assessment of power modules
  • Built in automated offline static and dynamic characterization platform for precise degradation monitoring
  • Customized stress testing platform to support statistical Design of Experiments (DoE)
  • Physics-of-failure oriented stress testing platform targeting thermomechanical fatigue and corrosion induced degradation in interconnects and metallization layers
