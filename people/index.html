Principal Investigator

Zhang Zheyu

  • Email: zhangz49@rpi.edu
  • Office: JEC 7018, 110 8th St., Troy, NY 12180
  • website: Google Scholar

    • Zheyu Zhang is an Assistant Professor and the inaugural recipient of the Richard P. Felak and Joan C. Felak Endowed Faculty Fellowship in Electric Power Engineering at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. He previously served as a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville (2015–2018), Lead Power Electronics Engineer at GE Research in Niskayuna, NY (2018–2019), and the Warren H. Owen – Duke Energy Assistant Professor of Engineering at Clemson University (2019–2023). Zhang’s research focuses on wide bandgap-based power electronics characterization and applications in electrified transportation, space power systems, energy storage, and renewable energy. He currently serves as Chair of the IEEE IAS Industrial Power Conversion Systems Department (IPCSD) Power Electronics Devices and Components Committee (PEDCC), and as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Power Electronics and IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications. His work has been recognized with four prize paper awards from the IEEE Industry Applications Society and the IEEE Power Electronics Society, the 2021 IEEE IAS Andrew W. Smith Outstanding Young Member Achievement Award, and the 2022 NASA Early Career Faculty Award. He is a Senior Member of IEEE.
